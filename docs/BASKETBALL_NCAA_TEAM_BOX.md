# NCAA team box archive

The [NCAA team box archive](/basketball/ncaa-team-box/) publishes source-derived season profiles for season-ending years 2010–11 through 2026–27. It uses the SportsDataverse `ncaa_team_box` parquet release, which is labeled CC BY 4.0, and retains the release URL, retrieval metadata and SHA-256 receipt for every season in R2.

Each attributed team-game record is grouped by the source NCAA team ID. The public profile sums source counts and recomputes offensive and defensive efficiency, net rating, tempo, effective field-goal percentage, true-shooting percentage, turnover rate, offensive-rebound rate, free-throw rate and three-point rate. Source averages and totals remain in each season edition for audit. The archive is descriptive and unadjusted; Silvermine's opponent-adjusted ratings and forecast model are separate products.

The browser supports season, program, minimum-games, metric and direction filters, shareable URLs and CSV export. A season reports unique source contests and team-game rows separately, so a contest involving two retained teams contributes two team-game rows while remaining one source contest.

The source release is a historical research input. Team names and IDs remain source-native, and the archive does not assert current membership, eligibility, transfer status or a cross-season identity join.
